# Golf bag

Superseded in part by plans/2026-10-10-club-distances.md: the bag is saved with SwiftData, not as a settings string, and each club has a distance.

The player picks the clubs they carry, in Settings. Later features use the bag to pick the club for a stroke, so the bag is a `Shared` model. Nothing reads it yet besides Settings.

## Clubs

Every club a player can carry, in bag order. Short names are what the buttons show.

| Kind | Clubs | Short names |
|---|---|---|
| Woods | Driver, 2 wood through 9 wood | `Dr`, `2w` … `9w` |
| Hybrids | 2 hybrid through 7 hybrid | `2h` … `7h` |
| Irons | 1 iron through 9 iron | `1i` … `9i` |
| Wedges | Pitching, approach (gap), sand and lob wedges, then 46° through 64° in 2° steps | `PW`, `AW`, `SW`, `LW`, `46°` … `64°` |
| Putter | Putter | `Pu` |

The default bag is the standard 14: driver, 3 wood, 5 wood, 3 iron through 9 iron, pitching, sand and lob wedges, and the putter.

## Model

`Shared/Models/Club.swift`:

| Type | Holds |
|---|---|
| `Club` | `id` (`"dr"`, `"3w"`, `"7i"`, `"pw"`, `"52"`, `"pu"`), `kind`, `shortName`, `name`. `Club.all` is every club in bag order |
| `ClubKind` | `wood`, `hybrid`, `iron`, `wedge`, `putter`, with a section title |
| `GolfBag` | The clubs carried, always in `Club.all` order. `toggle(_:)` adds or removes a club; adding to a full bag does nothing. `capacity` is 14 |

`GolfBag` is `RawRepresentable` with a `String` raw value, the club IDs joined by commas, so `@AppStorage` saves it directly. IDs it does not know are dropped when read.

## Settings

- `SettingsKey.bag`, default `GolfBag.standard.rawValue`. Reset with the other keys for UI tests.
- Settings gets a Clubs section with a "Golf Bag" link showing the count. It opens `GolfBagView`: one section per kind, each a grid of buttons. A button is filled when the club is in the bag, and tapping it adds or removes the club. When the bag holds 14, the buttons of clubs not in the bag are disabled. The header shows "n of 14 clubs".

## Tests

- `GolfBagTests`: the default bag, the raw value round trip, toggling, the full bag, unknown IDs, and that `Club.all` has no duplicate IDs.
- `GolfBagUITests`: opens the bag from Settings, sees 14 selected and a club disabled, removes one, adds one, and sees the count.
