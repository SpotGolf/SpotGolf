# App log

The watch's unified log rolls over within hours, so after the Legacy Ridge round of 2026-10-09 the first failure was gone, and reading what was left needed a sysdiagnose. From now on the apps keep their own log: every line is saved in the app's store, the watch sends its lines to the phone in the stream batches, and the phone shows and exports the lines of each round. Each line still goes to the unified log, so Xcode's console and Console.app work as before, and lines from before the store opens are not lost.

Builds on `feature/sensor-repair`: `AppVersion` in `Shared`, the version guard, and `SensorInputs`.

## Lines

A line is what the app logs today, as text, with where and when it was written.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `timestamp` | When the line was written, on that device's clock |
| `device` | `phone` or `watch` |
| `level` | `debug`, `notice`, `error` or `fault` |
| `category` | The `Log` category name, as today: `sensors`, `workout`, `sync`, … |
| `message` | The text. Everything is public: there is no `privacy:` any more |

`debug` is new. Lines at that level are saved only while the Debug Logging toggle is on; the other levels are always saved. Every level is also forwarded to `Logger`, where `debug` is not persisted by the system, as before.

Sizes stay small: a normal round is 300 to 600 lines, about 50 KB, and a round with a dead sensor restarting every 10 s for five hours is about 3,500 lines, under 350 KB. Nothing logs per sensor batch or per GPS fix, and debug lines must keep it that way.

## `Log`

`Log` keeps its static categories, but each becomes a `LogCategory` instead of a bare `Logger`:

```
Log.sensors.notice("accelerometer on")
  ─▶ logger.notice("\(text, privacy: .public)")        the unified log, as today
  ─▶ Log.sink.append(LogLine(...))                       the app's store
```

- `LogCategory` has `debug`, `notice`, `error` and `fault`, each taking a `String`. Call sites change from `"\(x, privacy: .public)"` to `"\(x)"`; about 160 of them, in `Shared`, `SpotGolf` and `SpotGolfWatch`.
- `debug` returns at once when `Log.debugEnabled` is false, before the string is built: callers pass an autoclosure.
- `Log.sink` is a `LogSink` set once at launch, after the store opens. Lines written before that, such as the store failing to open, are held in a buffer and handed to the sink when it is set. Writers are on sensor threads and sync queues, so the buffer is behind a lock and the sink hands lines to the main actor.
- `LogStore` is the sink in both apps. It coalesces lines and saves them once a second, and at once before a stream batch is built on the watch.

## Store

A SwiftData model, `LogEntry`, in both apps, added to `Storage.models`.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `roundID` | The round the line belongs to, or nil outside a round |
| `seq` | The line's position among its round's lines, or among the lines outside a round, from 0. The watch's lines keep the watch's `seq` on the phone |
| `timestamp`, `device`, `level`, `category`, `message` | As above |

`LogStore` is `@Observable`, like `StreamStore`, and has `append(_ lines:)`, `lines(for roundID:)`, `lines(outsideRounds:)`, `count(for:)`, `delete(_ roundID:)` and `export(_ roundID:)` (the text below).

Retention:

| Lines | Kept |
|---|---|
| In a round, phone | For the round's life, deleted with it by `RoundStore.delete` |
| In a round, watch | Until the phone has acknowledged them and the round's stream is deleted, by `StreamSender.deleteIfComplete` |
| Outside a round, both | The last 2,000. Trimmed after each save |
| Before a round, watch | Lines from the 5 minutes before a round starts are moved into the round, after the last watch line, so the workout launch that comes before the round is read with it. The same rule as `pendingEvents` |

On the phone, a line's round is the current round while one is in progress. On the watch it is the active round.

## Transport

The watch's lines travel in `StreamBatch` with the records, with their own cursor, so a lost batch or reply resends them and the phone never keeps a line twice.

```
StreamBatch   roundID, from, records          +  logsFrom, logs: [LogLine]
StreamAck     roundID, have                   +  haveLogs
```

- `StreamSender` keeps `logCursors` next to `cursors`. A batch carries the round's lines from the cursor, at most 200 or 20 KB, after the records. A round with pending lines and no pending records still sends, with empty records. The empty "ask" batch for a round with an unknown cursor learns both `have` and `haveLogs`.
- `StreamReceiver.receive` appends the lines past its `haveLogs`, with the same gap rule as records: a batch whose `logsFrom` is past `haveLogs` adds nothing and the ack tells the watch where to continue. Lines are saved before the ack goes, so `haveLogs` is always true.
- `deleteIfComplete` waits for both cursors to reach the end. An ack with no `haveLogs` (a phone from before this; the version guard prevents it, but the code must not hang) counts the lines as complete.
- The new fields are optional, so the message decodes on either side without them.
- The record kind `event` from `feature/sensor-repair` stays as it is: compact and in the CSV export. Dropping it is a later decision.

## Debug toggle

- `SettingsKey.debugLogging`, default false. Settings, Debug section: a Toggle "Debug Logging".
- The phone sends it in `StartRound.debugLogging`, and the watch keeps the last value it was sent in `UserDefaults`, so lines outside a round follow it too.

## Reading on the phone

Settings, Debug section, gets a "Log" link, and each past round gets a "Log" swipe action next to Export. Both open `LogView`.

| Part | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| List | Oldest first, as a log reads. Each row: time with milliseconds, a device mark, the category, the message. Errors and faults in red, debug lines in secondary color |
| Filter | A menu: device (both, phone, watch) and level (all, errors only). A search field over the message |
| Scope | From Settings: lines outside any round. From a round: that round's lines |
| Export | A share button writes `spotgolf-log-<round date or today>.txt` to the temporary directory and shows `ShareSheet`, like the track export |

Export line format, one line each:

```
2026-10-09T16:12:33.123Z watch E sensors accelerometer stopped on an error; restarting
```

Level letters: `D`, `N`, `E`, `F`.

## Tests

| Test | Checks |
|---|---|
| `LogTests` | `debug` is dropped when off and stored when on; lines written before the sink is set reach it; every level is forwarded |
| `LogStoreTests` | `seq` per round, device and outside rounds; the 2,000-line trim; delete with the round; the 5-minute move at round start; a queued line gets the round in progress and is saved on flush |
| `SyncCodecTests` | `StreamBatch` and `StreamAck` with and without the new fields |
| `SyncFlowTests` | Lines arrive in order and in capped batches; a lost reply resends and the phone keeps no duplicate; a gap is refused; the watch deletes a round's lines and stream only when both cursors are complete; an ack without `haveLogs` completes; a resumed round continues the numbering; pre-round lines go with the round; the debug setting reaches the watch; a cancelled round's lines go on both devices |
| `LogUITests` | Settings opens the log and the launch line is listed; the share sheet shows the file; a past round's swipe action opens its lines |

Then a round on the real phone and watch, with Debug Logging on, read on the phone before the round ends.

## Version

A `feat`, so MINOR. Nothing older builds saved is read differently.

## Out of scope

- Removing the `event` record kind.
- Sending the phone's lines to the watch.
- Log lines in the CSV export.
