# SpotGolf

A simple golf ball tracking app for iOS and watchOS.

## Overview

SpotGolf helps golfers keep track of where their balls land on the course. The core interaction is a single button — **"At my ball"** — that records your GPS location when you reach your ball.

### iOS
- Map view with pins showing where you marked each ball during a round
- Start and manage rounds to keep your marks organized

### watchOS
- Quick "At my ball" button right on your wrist
- Shows distance from your previous mark (how far you hit it)
- Syncs marks to the iPhone app via WatchConnectivity

## Tech Stack

- SwiftUI
- CoreLocation
- MapKit
- WatchConnectivity
